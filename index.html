Reference values

Coefficients are copied from win_rate_params in src/uci.cpp at tag sf_19, the Stockfish 19 release of 5 September 2026. The score the engine prints is round(100 · v / a), so cp and WDL are two views of one internal value. The development branch carries different coefficients, so results here match release builds only.

Mate and tablebase scores are not converted. Win and loss use 1 / (1 + exp((a − v) / b)), draw is 1000 − W − L, each rounded as the engine does.
